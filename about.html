My Story

Who I Am

My name is Sami Khan Prithvy, and I am a first-year BSc Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence student at Bangor University, Wales, UK. Born and raised in Dhaka, Bangladesh, I built a strong academic foundation at Engineering University School and College, where I achieved a GPA of 4.00 and earned distinctions in ICT, Mathematics, and the Sciences. Moving to the United Kingdom to pursue my degree has been a defining chapter in my life — one that has pushed me to grow not only as a student, but as an individual. I bring with me hands-on experience in Python programming, front-end web development, and Object-Oriented Programming, all of which I continue to build upon through my studies and personal projects.

I am deeply passionate about the intersection of technology and real-world problem solving. Whether it is automating a repetitive task with Python, designing a clean and accessible web interface, or exploring how artificial intelligence can reshape industries, I find genuine excitement in building things that work and matter. Beyond the screen, I enjoy gaming — which has sharpened my strategic thinking and quick decision-making — and I take pride in my ability to communicate and connect with people across cultures, being fluent in English, Bengali, Urdu, and Hindi. These experiences have given me a unique perspective that I bring into every project and collaboration I am part of.

Looking ahead, I aspire to build a career at the forefront of Artificial Intelligence and software development, contributing to solutions that are innovative, inclusive, and impactful. I am particularly excited about the possibilities within cloud computing, Python automation, and intelligent systems. I am actively seeking internship and placement opportunities where I can apply what I have learned, grow within a professional environment, and make a meaningful contribution. I believe that the best technology is built by curious, driven, and collaborative people — and that is exactly the kind of professional I am working every day to become.
